About Justin Lott

Justin Lott is a portrait photographer with a passion for capturing authentic moments and meaningful connections. His love for photography grew through years of photographing family and children. The image below, from Sand Hollow, is an example of his contest winning work.

Justin draws much of his inspiration from the outdoors. He was raised on a farm in the scenic mountains of northeastern Washington, where he developed a deep appreciation for nature and open spaces. Now based in Utah, he finds inspiration while hiking along the benches of northern Utah and exploring the striking landscapes of the state’s southern parks.
