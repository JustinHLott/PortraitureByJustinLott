Photographer Justin Lott

I am a portrait photographer with a passion for capturing authentic moments and meaningful connections. My love for photography grew through years of photographing my family and children. The image below, from Sand Hollow, is an example of my contest winning work.

I draw much of his inspiration from the outdoors. I was raised on a farm in the scenic mountains of northeastern Washington, where I developed a deep appreciation for nature and open spaces. Now based in Utah, I find inspiration while hiking along the benches of northern Utah and exploring the striking landscapes of the state’s southern parks.
